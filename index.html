A café built around the first cup of the day

Brew & Bloom began in 2017 as a two-table counter and a secondhand roaster. We still buy directly from small farms in Coorg, Chikmagalur and Ethiopia, and we pay above fair-trade prices.

Everything is made in small batches: beans roasted twice a week, milk steamed to order, croissants laminated by hand. The flowers on every table are from the market down the road.
